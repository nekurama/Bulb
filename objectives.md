# NEKURAMA Company Objectives

## Status

This document records the proposed objectives and business activities for the
proposed NEKURAMA private limited company. The company name, incorporation
status, object clause, and regulatory treatment remain subject to confirmation
by the appointed CS, CA, and legal counsel.

## Primary objective

The company will build and operate technology products and services for small
and medium-sized businesses. Its initial product will be **BABAI**, a B2B SaaS
platform focused initially on restaurants and local businesses.

BABAI is intended to help businesses manage customer conversations,
menus/catalogues, orders, pickup and fulfilment workflows, staff operations,
business communications, analytics, and related business processes through
WhatsApp and web/cloud software.

The platform may use artificial intelligence for assistance, information
extraction, recommendations, summarisation, and workflow support. Authoritative
business actions will remain controlled by defined software rules and
authorised users.

## Proposed business activities

The company may:

1. Research, design, develop, license, host, maintain, support, and
   commercialise software, SaaS, web, mobile, cloud, AI, data, automation, and
   communication products.
2. Provide implementation, integration, configuration, training, technical
   support, analytics, and related technology services.
3. Develop APIs and integrations with messaging, payment, cloud, business, and
   other technology providers, subject to applicable laws and provider
   requirements.
4. Own, license, protect, and commercialise software, technology, brands,
   trademarks, domain names, documentation, and other intellectual property.
5. Enter into lawful partnerships, vendor arrangements, reseller arrangements,
   and other activities incidental or ancillary to the technology business
   described above.

## Initial operating boundaries

Initially, the company does not intend to:

- Operate restaurants as its principal business.
- Provide food delivery as a principal business.
- Hold customer funds or operate a wallet or escrow service.
- Provide financial services or act as a payment intermediary without the
  required authorisation.
- Act as a telecom operator.

Customer payments are intended to settle directly to the relevant business
through authorised payment providers.

## Professional review required

The appointed CS, CA, and legal counsel should review and finalise the
primary and ancillary object clauses, including:

- MCA filing and permitted-object wording.
- Entity, director, founder, and shareholder requirements.
- Tax, GST, accounting, and incentive implications.
- Payment, data protection, AI, messaging, and technology-provider
  obligations.
- Intellectual-property ownership, licensing, and employment restrictions.

This document describes the intended business scope and is not a legal,
tax, regulatory, or filing opinion.
