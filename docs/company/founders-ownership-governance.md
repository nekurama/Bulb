## Incorporation transition

Before the incorporation certificate, prepare but do not treat the following as executed company arrangements:

- founder/shareholder agreement;
- vesting, cliff, leaver, transfer, reserved-matters, and deadlock terms;
- founder/company adoption and IP/confidentiality documents; and
- material contracts, hiring, or formal company commitments.

Immediately after the certificate, execute the founder/shareholder agreement and complete the company adoption documents **before material contracts**. CS/lawyer review is required for the sequence and terms.

## Director and approval governance

- Review the proposed unpaid non-executive director structure with the CS/lawyer.
- Review Manoj’s Qualcomm outside-directorship, conflict-of-interest, invention/IP, confidentiality, and disclosure policies before accepting any director or company role.
- Unpaid status does not remove statutory director duties or other obligations; do not infer a legal exemption.
- Use hybrid approvals: a single authorised founder may approve routine low-value expenses within a documented limit; dual approval is required for material payments, contracts, hiring, IP transfers, and banking or account-control changes.
