## Founder and research evidence

The current market evidence is directional, not conclusive:

- `nekurama.babai.research.md`, **Summary**: 19 businesses were interviewed (15 restaurants and 4 stores). Feedback was strongly positive, with reported dissatisfaction with aggregators, interest in direct customer relationships, existing WhatsApp ordering, and difficulty managing conversations.
- `nekurama.babai.research.md`, **Customer pain observed**: Rasaangi reported approximately ₹1.57 lakh in aggregator sales but ₹54,000 received; Chantabbai reported approximately ₹15 lakh generated but ₹8 lakh received; Brew Vista Cafe wanted to leave aggregators because remaining margin was insufficient.
- `nekurama.babai.research.md`, **Customer pain observed**: a local store already receives WhatsApp messages, but staff management and English-language conversations are difficult.
- `nekurama.babai.research.md`, **What remains unvalidated**: willingness to provide WhatsApp access and menus, change operating habits, use the system during busy periods, pay subscription/onboarding fees, trust automation, and continue after trial remain unproven.
- `nekurama.raw.chat.json`, message index 73 (mapping order; no separate message-index field): founder/history discussion describes the ICP as “WhatsApp operational pain + direct-order potential + business-owned customer relationship + economic dissatisfaction where commission platforms are involved.”

These observations support restaurant-first discovery and founder-led validation; they do not establish market size, repeatable acquisition, competitive superiority, or a permanent exclusion of stores and other local businesses.
