## Governance model

- **Operational decisions:** delegated to the responsible function owner.
- **Board decisions:** major corporate, strategic and risk decisions.
- **Reserved matters:** material dilution or share issuance, sale/licensing of core IP, major
  debt, acquisition/disposal, subsidiaries/JVs, related-party transactions, fundamental business
  changes and changes to founder rights.
- **Founder protection:** vesting/lock-in, leaver treatment, deadlock resolution and transfer
  restrictions require drafting; none is implied by this page.
- **Employment constraint:** Manoj's Qualcomm restrictions must be reviewed before any director,
  officer, employee or IP-assignment arrangement is executed.
