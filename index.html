01A / WHY NEKURAMA

Technology should return attention.

NEKURAMA is being shaped around useful, governed software for people who run real businesses—not another layer of noise.

THE MOTIVATIONMake complex work feel human.Clear states, visible handoffs and recovery instead of hidden automation.
THE BELIEFOwn the relationship, not just the transaction.Businesses should keep context, trust and a direct path to their customers.
THE STANDARDUseful before impressive.Every promise must survive operational, economic and human review.

01B / NEKURAMA PRODUCTS

One operating idea. Many useful products.

NEKURAMA is the parent direction. BABAI is the first product being explored in this prototype; future products must earn their place through the same discipline.

NEKURAMAParent company and operating identity.Builds and owns focused products with clear boundaries.
BABAIBusiness automation by AI for restaurants.WhatsApp-first ordering, pickup and human-assisted operations.
THE PRODUCT RULEBounded automation, visible control.No unrestricted AI authority, hidden data use or silent handoff.

01C / BABAI INTRO

A calmer way for restaurants to take orders.

BABAI connects the customer conversation to the restaurant operation while keeping the staff, the order and the next action visible.

RECOVER DEMANDTurn missed messages into structured order intent.Menu, cart, quantity and pickup context stay together for review.
PROTECT THE RELATIONSHIPKeep the direct customer path with the restaurant.Pickup-first flows avoid forcing every customer through another marketplace experience.
SAVE STAFF ATTENTIONAutomate the routine, preserve the human.When a question needs judgment, the collected context moves with the handoff.
MAKE RECOVERY VISIBLEFailures become states to resolve, not surprises to hide.Payment, provider and fulfillment boundaries remain explicit.
