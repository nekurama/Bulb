# BABAI Knowledge Base

BABAI is NEKURAMA's first product/brand.

## Current product truth

BABAI is a **B2B business operating platform built around the business's own WhatsApp identity**. It helps restaurants run customer and operational workflows through bounded AI, automation and human intervention. The current promise is:

> **Run your business without leaving WhatsApp.**

Restaurants are the initial wedge. The first product proof is a narrow, pickup-first restaurant workflow—not a consumer marketplace, POS/ERP replacement, delivery network or general-purpose AI assistant. Longer-term expansion to other local businesses is a direction, not yet validated scope.

## Current validation boundary

The MVP is a thin, restaurant-first, pickup-first, WhatsApp-native workflow.
The current commercial validation is a **fixed 90-day paid pilot**, not a free
trial. Cancellation, refund and minimum-paying terms belong in a signed
agreement template; the legal wording and exact terms remain open. The pilot
ends at the 90-day readiness review when team-defined evidence shows BABAI is
operationally ready for materially larger onboarding volumes. There is no
automatic extension or pricing increase decision in this artifact.
