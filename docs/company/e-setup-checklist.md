# NEKURAMA Company E-Setup Checklist

This is the execution sequence for the company workstreams. It records the admin decision packet without inventing legal, tax, employment, or regulatory conclusions.

## 1. Before the incorporation certificate

- [ ] Preserve the Private Limited application record and monitor the incorporation certificate.
- [ ] Do not execute major contracts, hire, or formally transfer IP before incorporation without CS/lawyer advice.
- [ ] Prepare, but do not treat as executed, the founder/shareholder agreement, four-year vesting with one-year cliff, founder/company adoption documents, worker terms, approval matrix, and account-migration plan.
- [ ] Perform NEKURAMA/BABAI name and trademark clearance with trademark counsel.
- [ ] Review Manoj’s Qualcomm outside-directorship, conflict, disclosure, confidentiality, and employer-IP policies.
- [ ] Inventory founder-owned domain, GitHub, AWS, Meta, billing, and recovery accounts without copying credentials into company docs.

## 2. Incorporation certificate transition

- [ ] **CS:** attach the certificate and company identifiers; confirm the registered-office and director records.
- [ ] **Founders + CS/lawyer:** execute the founder/shareholder agreement immediately after incorporation and before material contracts.
- [ ] **Founders + lawyer/CS:** execute company adoption, IP assignment/licence, confidentiality, and background-IP schedules.
- [ ] **CS/lawyer:** review the unpaid non-executive director structure and duties; unpaid status is not a duty exemption.
- [ ] **Founders:** document Manoj 55% / Vinay 45% and the four-year/one-year-cliff vesting terms in the approved instruments.

## 3. Operating controls after incorporation

- [ ] **Governance:** adopt hybrid approvals: one authorised founder for routine low-value expenses within a written limit; dual approval for material payments, contracts, hiring, transfers, and banking changes.
- [ ] **Finance + CA/bookkeeper:** start books from incorporation using controlled accounting software and monthly reconciliation.
- [ ] **CA:** confirm GST/tax setup and retain filing/registration evidence.
- [ ] **Incentives owner:** assess DPIIT, T-Hub, and incubator applications only when eligibility evidence supports them; track DeepTech/80-IAC/state schemes separately.
- [ ] **Security owner:** migrate domain, GitHub, AWS, Meta, billing, and recovery accounts to company control with MFA and audited recovery.
- [ ] **Legal owner:** execute material customer/vendor/worker contracts only after the incorporation and approval gates pass.

## 4. Deferred but planned

- [ ] **Founders + counsel:** defer ESOP issuance until key hires or funding justify it, while preserving a plan for future implementation.
- [ ] **Founders:** defer subsidiaries/group structure until a concrete legal, economic, ownership, regulatory, or operating trigger.
- [ ] **Manager:** keep BABAI product architecture, privacy UX, tenant controls, and web follow-ups in product documentation.

## Professional validation register

| Item | Required reviewer |

| Qualcomm outside-directorship, conflicts, confidentiality, employer IP | Employer policy owner + lawyer |

## Evidence rule

Each checked item requires an artefact: certificate, signed agreement, counsel/CA confirmation, clearance report, account-control record, software/access record, reconciliation, filing, or application evidence. Never use a credential or unverified screenshot as evidence.
