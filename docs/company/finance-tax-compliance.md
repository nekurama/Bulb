# Finance, Tax & Compliance

## Current answer

Company finance must distinguish founder capital, expenses/reimbursements, company operating revenue, SaaS revenue, customer payment flows, provider charges, taxes and FX.

BABAI customer payments to restaurants are not company revenue/custody by default. BABAI subscription billing is separate from restaurant order settlement.

Current company opportunity/compliance direction includes DPIIT recognition and SIPP/IPR support. DeepTech status and 80-IAC eligibility need professional validation. GeM is optional. ONDC is deliberately not an immediate company workstream, while architecture remains aware of it. Central schemes belong in an opportunity register.
