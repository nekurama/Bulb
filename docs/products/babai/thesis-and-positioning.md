# Thesis & Positioning

## Definition

> BABAI is a business operating platform built around WhatsApp. It helps businesses run their customer and operational workflows through AI and automation.

## Wedge

Restaurant-first MVP. Restaurants are the initial learning wedge, not a permanent category boundary.

## Long-term direction

WhatsApp-native B2B2C local-business operating/commerce layer, potentially extending to marts, general stores, medical shops and other repeat-purchase local businesses after restaurant evidence.

## What BABAI is not

- Not a consumer marketplace.
- Not a Swiggy/Zomato intermediary.
- Not a POS/ERP replacement.
- Not merely a WhatsApp API wrapper.
- Not a generic AI chatbot.

## Core principle

If an operation can reasonably be performed through WhatsApp, the user should not be forced into a web/app flow.

## Strategic boundary

WhatsApp-first, not WhatsApp-only. Web exists for dense configuration, analytics, comparison, bulk actions, recovery and multi-branch administration.
