## Control boundary

The register distinguishes:

- **Repository decision:** the owner, dependency, evidence type, and next action recorded here.
- **Outside-chat control:** a certificate, signed agreement, professional opinion, filing, provider approval, or account state held by the relevant external party.

The repository must not turn “the CA is handling it,” “the application was submitted,” or “the provider is available” into a completion claim. Each item remains pending until an appropriate external artefact is available.

## Sequencing

1. Before the incorporation certificate, preserve the application and prepare reviews; do not treat major contracts, hiring, or formal IP transfer as complete.
2. After the certificate, complete the founder/shareholder and company adoption documents with CS/lawyer review before material contracts.
3. Use the finance, incentive, provider, and account-control evidence to update this register and the relevant company checklist.

See [`e-setup-checklist.md`](e-setup-checklist.md) for the broader transition checklist and [`README.md`](README.md) for company status conventions.
