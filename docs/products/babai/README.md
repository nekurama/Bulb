## Current product truth

BABAI is a business operating platform built around WhatsApp. It helps businesses run customer and operational workflows through AI and automation.

Initial wedge: restaurants. Long-term direction: a WhatsApp-native B2B2C local-business operating/commerce layer, subject to evidence.
